What the looper is for

Play a bass line into track 1 and let it repeat. Add a countermelody on track 2. Try a different bass line in another slot, then switch between the two while the melody keeps going. The looper records the notes and their timing. On playback it sends MIDI messages to your synths or software instruments.

Each of the eight tracks can play one of its eight slots. A slot holds a phrase and its undo history. A set contains all eight tracks. A milestone is a saved version of the set that you can recall later. Capturing an entire jam as a song arrangement is a longer-term project goal.
