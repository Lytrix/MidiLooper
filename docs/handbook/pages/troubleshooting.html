The track buttons respond, but the synth is silent

Check the control pads and the keyboard separately. A track pad sends a note on MIDI channel 16 to select a track. The keys you play should transmit on channels 1–12. For a test, select track 1 and play MIDI note 60 on channel 1. Connect your synth to DIN OUT and set it to receive on channel 1, or route USB MIDI channel 1 to a software instrument. Listen to the instrument’s audio output.

If you can hear your keyboard but the recorded loop is silent, check the track’s mute and solo settings. Soloing another track can silence the recorded part.

The record indicator flashes, but the take has not started

A flashing record indicator means the slot is armed. If the transport is stopped, press global PLAY/STOP. During playback, the take may be waiting for the next bar or the end of the selected track’s loop. Watch the countdown. The looper records or overdubs one track at a time, so finish any other take first.

Tapping a track button mutes it

A short EDIT press switches the eight track buttons between track selection and mute. Tap it again to return to track selection. To enter NOTE_EDIT on a recorded loop, hold EDIT for about 0.6 seconds and release before 1.5 seconds. A 1.5-second hold exits editing. On an empty slot the shorter hold opens LOOP_EDIT.

Only part of the loop plays back

The brackets on the progress bar show the current loop range. Shortening the range can leave recorded notes outside it. In performance or LOOP_EDIT, double-tap NOTELEN to hear the full loop again. For a single missing note, check its velocity in NOTE_EDIT. Raise the velocity above zero to hear it.

Will my take be there when I power up again?

Finish the take, leave NOTE_EDIT and wait for the four save dots to flash bright. If you see RO, the looper cannot write to the card. Check the card setup. To keep a version of all eight tracks that you can recall later, choose SAVE SET and wait for confirmation. If the display says BUSY, wait for the current save or load to finish. See Save & load sets for card space and other messages.

I selected another slot, but the old loop is still playing

During playback, tap a recorded slot once to queue it for the end of the current loop. Double-tap the new slot to launch it on the next division of the launch grid. The flashing playhead shows where playback will start. With the transport stopped, a single tap selects the slot for the next start.

My pad sends the right note number, but the control does nothing

Check the MIDI channel as well as the note number. Set the pad to send Note On on channel 16 when pressed and Note Off when released. Its LED may receive a different note number on channel 15. For a fader, check which bank is active and whether the assignment uses Control Change or Pitch Bend. Find the exact message in the MIDI control map.
