## Bounds and controls
 - Reuse the existing `Track::processDeferredIdleMaintenance` Snapshot owner during RECORDING only when its live-record work is already eligible, and continue it after stop through the same owner. Each turn advances at most the existing bounded Snapshot construction unit; no partial candidate becomes current, and MIDI/record admission and deferred persistence are not delayed or gated.
 - Reuse the existing `Track::stepPlaybackViewMaintenance` holder-selection/admission path and three prewarmed playback views. Record-source eligibility must be integrated into that path; do not add direct side service, a second selector, or a reserved turn. Repeatedly prepare one complete bar ahead; whole-loop root remains single and atomic.
 - A successor is immutable once ready and atomically adopted. The current immutable view remains the sole serving view until adoption.
 - Keep Snapshot and deferred persistence eligible concurrently. No `stop`, wait-for-root on the clock edge, inline whole-loop build, blanket silence, or user-visible re-press.
 - An accepted overdub interaction is never gated on lookahead. The maintenance turn yields at its existing grain boundary; source identity/generation guards prevent stale candidate publication/adoption, preserve the last complete serving view, and leave the overdub and Snapshot continuations owned by their existing paths. No new lock, queue, or ownership transfer is introduced.
 - Each owner turn advances at most one existing fill/order grain; existing holder fairness and cursor behavior remain observable. The handoff deadline is the next interval entry; supported service-gap/interference limits remain S7.5a's job, not guessed here.
