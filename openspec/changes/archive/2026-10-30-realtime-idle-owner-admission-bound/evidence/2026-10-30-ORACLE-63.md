## Conclusion

The bundle split is correct and proven, but the remaining persistence bar breach is the **LoopPersist
finalize/rename**, which is on the timing-critical lane and is not bounded by the between-step budget.
6.3 stays open; row 14 stays coarse/empirical. The next decision is whether to defer the loop
finalize while the transport runs (the mid-pass capture-chunk flush remains the safety net) or to
instrument the finalize sub-stage first.
