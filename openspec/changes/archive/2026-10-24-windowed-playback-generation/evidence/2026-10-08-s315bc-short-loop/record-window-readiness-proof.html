Can late Record input be ready on a one-bar loop?

Under the currently declared owner-service contract: no universal next-traversal guarantee. A persistent complete current window solves unchanged wraps, but not new content arriving after the last safe preparation opportunity.

Proved impossible under the current contract

A changed interval has at most 768 ticks before the next one-bar traversal. The current canCompleteWithinLead predicate requires 1,792 ticks of available lead for an empty successor and 2,304 ticks for a two-event successor, before extra source work. These are conservative admission thresholds, not measured build durations or proof that hardware could never be faster.

Admission inequality

Available ≥ margin + grains × holders × advance

Current terms: margin 768 ticks; eight cross-track opportunities per grain (seven denied + one served); permitted advance 64 ticks. Source: Track::playbackViewCoverageContract and CoverageServiceContract::canCompleteWithinLead.

Adversarial arrival

An accepted Record event arrives just before the end of its one-bar cycle. It cannot already be in a frozen immutable window. Its next occurrence is at most 768 ticks away; a private successor must incorporate it before that tick.

Eight sounding tracks: necessary capacity

Eight simultaneous two-event changes cost at least 8 × 3 = 24 owner grains. One-grain-per-turn service must guarantee 24 opportunities inside 768 ticks: at most 32 ticks/opportunity with zero margin, or about 21 ticks/opportunity with a 256-tick margin. The declared permitted gap is 64 ticks. Faster scheduling still needs CPU and contention proof.

Stop-time geometry is separate

Stop selects final loop length and may project raw coverage or close held notes. A prepared window transfers unchanged only if its Capture generation, geometry, content and closures match. Otherwise the frozen source must remain pinned while the owner prepares a corrected successor.

Conditions for a real guarantee

  1. Keep a complete immutable current per sounding slot, with a private build and complete ready. A late Capture append invalidates the candidate source identity, never the reader.
  2. Prove a bounded incremental successor and service cadence. For the earliest affected deadline D, establish M + (G + K) × H × Δ ≤ D, including source lookup, ordering, closures, publication and competing holders. Today's one-bar margin alone consumes the deadline.
  3. On failure, name the missed interval and retain the previous complete source for only its permitted traversal. One-pass-late is containment, not a readiness pass; do not discard accepted input to make the bound pass.
  4. Verify the real production entry → deferred owner → clock reader with native interleavings, then measure worst-case service on a pinned device image under eight-track contention, late append, stop, persistence and display work.

Conditional proof: if a complete current exists, accepted changes retain their source, all charged work is finite, and measured service satisfies the inequality for each affected deadline, the owner finishes ready before that deadline. The edge then adopts it. Repeated edits must preserve useful progress instead of indefinitely restarting it. Those new bounds are not established today.

Decision before cutover

The present contract cannot promise next-traversal readiness for arbitrarily late accepted Record input on a one-bar loop. Qualify a stronger bounded service protocol, or explicitly change the musical latency requirement. Reusing unchanged current solves wraps, not late mutations.
