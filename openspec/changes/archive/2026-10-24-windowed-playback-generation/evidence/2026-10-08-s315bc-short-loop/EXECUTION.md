## G2 feasibility finding — distinguish served current from changed successor

The existing `Track::playbackViewCoverageContract` sets `maximumPhaseAdvancePerTurn=64`, `maximumConsecutiveDeniedTurns=7` (eight tracks) and `leadTicks=768`. Its predicate therefore requires **1,792 ticks of available lead** for a newly built empty window (one fill + one promotion grain) and **2,304 ticks** for a two-event window (one fill + one order + one promotion grain). `test_one_bar_short_loop_does_not_bypass_the_declared_owner_deadline` asserts 1,792 exactly and that the two-event one-bar request is refused; 2,304 is derived from the production order-grain formula, not separately asserted. These are conservative **current admission thresholds, not measured build times**. The 768-tick `leadTicks` is a chosen one-bar margin from the earlier window-owner slice, not a product requirement for an already complete reader. The existing rolling requester also hardcodes two-bar coverage and excludes short loops; a guard-only relaxation is not a solution.

**Refined transition contract (design input; no timing claim):** a complete immutable, source-qualified `current` is immediately servable on an unchanged wrap without another request or an extra fixed bar of lead. Accepted late Record input changes the source: the *remaining* bounded work for its private successor must fit the earliest affected playback tick under all active holders, with a separately justified residual margin `M`. Charge `G_remaining` and same-track siblings `K` through the measured service terms: `M + (G_remaining + K) × H × Δ ≤ D_affected`. Even with `M=0`, the current two/three-grain builds at H=8 and Δ=64 need **1,024/1,536 ticks**, more than a 768-tick next traversal. Therefore removing the 768-tick margin alone does not qualify late one-bar changes. The preferred path is to cold-prepare first `current`, incrementally preserve useful Capture-window work while Record advances, and qualify a bounded final successor step and/or stronger owner opportunity under contention. Final stop geometry and closures remain charged. Until the production path, native mutation and pinned hardware service prove this contract, keep old emitters as migration fallback and name missed deadlines; no supported-route cutover or 3.15b/c — single-path migration checkoff follows.
